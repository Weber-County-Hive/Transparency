What this is about

Utah is being asked to host giant data centers. They are sold on two things: tax money and jobs. This case file checks the two biggest Great Salt Lake proposals against their own papers. Stratos is in Box Elder County. Tooele Valley is in Tooele County.

The full case file comes in two parts. Part 1 covers the projects and the promises. Part 2 covers AI, jobs and who is keeping count.

Part 1: The promise and the paper

Stratos was promoted on 2,000 permanent jobs. Outside estimates are lower.

Permanent jobs at Stratos

Promise from Kevin O'Leary. Estimates as cited in the full case file. The industry range is staff for 1 gigawatt of computing.

Promised (first gigawatt)
2,000
Outside estimate (USC)
~1,350
Industry range at 1 GW
200–400

The draft agreements require a big power plant within five years. In the parts the Hive read, they set no number of jobs. If terms are not met, the only fix in the drafts is a lawsuit to make the other side follow the deal. No money damages can be collected. The yearly job count must be reported, but it can be marked confidential.

These agreements are still drafts. MIDA's board approved them in April 2026 but has not signed them. Only two documents are signed: the project area and a 0.5% energy tax.

The money also flows in many directions. Box Elder County is promised upfront payments and a yearly minimum. Most of the main building payment goes back to the company and to the developer. Data center equipment pays no sales tax, even when it is replaced.

The computer chips inside wear out in three to six years. The deals around the buildings last 25 to 50 years. Nothing the Hive read says what happens to the buildings if the campus closes.

New since we posted: On Oct. 15, 2026, Stratos filed for water rights. That filing lists 4,850 employees. It is a water filing, not one of the MIDA agreements.

Part 2: Who is counting?

Employers are naming AI more often when they cut jobs.

U.S. job cuts where employers named AI

Challenger, Gray & Christmas. Reasons employers gave, not proven causes.

All of 2025 (12 months)
54,836
Jan.–Aug. 2026 (8 months)
116,175

By the end of September 2026, that count reached 120,136. A world forecast says new technology will make more jobs than it removes by 2030. But they are often different jobs that need different training.

The unemployment rate misses many people. If you stop looking, you are not counted. If you take a job below your training, you count as employed. No one counts qualified people who applied and never got an interview.

The Hive found no Utah count of jobs AI has replaced or created, and no record of how many data center workers are local.

What you can ask for

A job number in the agreement. A way to get money back if the jobs don't come. Job counts the public can see. A cleanup plan for when the technology moves on.
